# Reminder Mail

- `NoteReminderMail.php` uses the `mail.note-reminder` view and receives a portable note URL.
- Keep the subject and body generic. Never include a note title, content, or other private note data.
- See [0005-note-reminders.md](../../docs/specs/0005-note-reminders.md) for the delivery and privacy requirements.
